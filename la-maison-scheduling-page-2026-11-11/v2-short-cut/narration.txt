There are five tabs across the top: My dates, Tasks, Supplies, Contractors, and Help. Here's what each one does for you. My dates is where you choose the days you'd like to work at La Maison. Signing up takes three quick steps, and they're listed right here at the top. Every date gets its own card. A grey card with a lock means someone already has that day. A white card is open for you. See the orange word Needed? That job is still available. Each card tells you the date, the hours, and who else is working that day. Some cards also say same-day turnover, right next to the hours. Found a day you like? Tap the card. It turns green and gets a check mark. Pick as many dates as you want, all in one visit. Now the important part: tap Sign me up. That tap puts your name on the La Maison calendar. A check mark alone doesn't save your spot. If you leave without tapping Sign me up, the date stays open. Once you're signed up, your name shows on that day's calendar event, from checkout to check-in. You'll also see a calendar with all of your dates on it. Plans change, and that's fine. Uncheck the date, tap Sign me up again, and it opens up for someone else. Inspectors, your link works the same way. Your white cards are the dates that still need an inspection. Inspectors, the Tasks tab is your checklist for every visit. Please go through the whole list every time, even if the house looked great last visit. There are seventy-six items, sorted into thirteen groups like Bathroom, Kitchen, and Living Areas. Tap a group to open it. The numbers on the right keep track of how far you've gotten. Finished an item? Tap the circle next to it. Curious why an item is on the list? Tap Instructions and why it matters. Finished items get a check mark and a line through them, and your count goes up. At the bottom, you can see how many items you've completed. All done? Tap Submit inspection, and your results go straight to Slack. If you see a Your name box above the button, type your name first. Running low on something at the house? That's what the Supplies tab is for. Tap the first box and pick the item from the list, or type its name. Then say how much is left, like two rolls or half a bottle. Tap Add Item to List, and it goes on the list of things to buy. Need the pool service or the handyman? The Contractors tab has La Maison's maintenance vendors. Search by name, type, phone, or email to find the right one quickly. Then tap Text or Email to reach them directly. And if you ever forget a step, the Help tab has it all in writing. That's everything. Thank you for taking such good care of La Maison.
